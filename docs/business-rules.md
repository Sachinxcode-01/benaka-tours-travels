# Kedar Tours & Travels — Business Rules & Operational Specifications

This document defines the single source of truth for business operations, fleet policies, and contact information for **Kedar Tours & Travels**.

---

## 1. Core Operating Principles

1. **Strictly 100% Chauffeur-Driven**: Self-drive car rentals are strictly NOT offered under any circumstances. Every rental includes a certified, experienced, uniformed chauffeur.
2. **Zero Customer Damage Liability**: Operational vehicle liability and maintenance rests exclusively with the designated chauffeur.
3. **No Static Public Pricing**: Fixed per-day or flat rates are not published; trip prices are calculated dynamically based on distance (km), duration (days/hours), route geography, toll expectations, driver allowances, and vehicle category.
4. **Primary Lead & Inquiry Channels**: All bookings transition seamlessly into direct WhatsApp inquiry messages or telephone calls.
5. **Official Branded Quotations**: Customers and corporate clients receive branded estimate receipts (`KTT-QUOTE-YYYY-XXXX`) detailing route, dates, vehicle specs, and estimated cost breakdown.
